# 06: Plan, edit, commit, and cancel purchases

**What to build:** The owner records expected or committed purchases and understands their effect on available and projected funds.

**Blocked by:** 03: Configure annual budgets and see available funds.

**Status:** draft — pending testing-seam and ticket-breakdown review

- [ ] Require anticipated date and allow easy edits of amount, description, and categories.
- [ ] Preserve original estimate and revision history.
- [ ] Expected affects projected balance only; committed also reduces available funds.
- [ ] Cancel plans without deleting them; hide canceled plans by default and exclude them from balances.
- [ ] Explicitly move outstanding plans between years without automatically rolling unused budgets forward.
- [ ] Verify state changes and balances through shared operations and a usable plan list.
